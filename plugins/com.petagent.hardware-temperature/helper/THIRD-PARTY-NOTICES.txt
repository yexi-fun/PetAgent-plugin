This plugin bundles LibreHardwareMonitor 0.9.6 and its runtime dependencies.

LibreHardwareMonitor and the bundled libraries are distributed under their
respective licenses. The LibreHardwareMonitor source and license are available
at https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor.

The bundled PawnIO_setup.exe is distributed by LibreHardwareMonitor as an
optional resource and is used only to install the PawnIO driver with explicit
administrator consent.
